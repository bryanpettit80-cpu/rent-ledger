Rent Ledger Privacy Notice

Effective July 30, 2026

Rent Ledger is a personal rent-administration tool operated by the person who deployed this copy. It is a static browser app, not a public account service. The app has no developer-operated backend, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data kept in this browser

The app stores landlord settings, tenant contact and rental details, invoices, charges, payments, closed periods, audit events, and backup history in this browser's local storage. It also creates invoice PDFs, CSV reports, and JSON backups in the browser when requested.

Browser data remains until it is deleted in the app or the browser's site data is cleared. Anyone who can use this browser profile or run code on the same website origin may be able to access that local data.

Optional Google Drive use

If the operator connects Google Drive, Rent Ledger requests the limited drive.file permission. The app can upload and download its state file and invoice PDFs in a visible Rent Ledger folder. Google retains those files under the connected account's settings and policies.

Optional Google Email use

If the operator connects Google Email, Rent Ledger requests permission to identify the connected Google account and send email through Gmail. It does not request permission to read the inbox, contacts, or existing messages.

After the operator reviews and presses Send, the app sends the displayed sender, recipient, subject, and message body to the Gmail API. New-invoice and payment-reminder messages also include the invoice PDF shown in the review dialog. Payment-received messages are text only. Rent Ledger supplies the complete message body and does not add the operator's normal Gmail signature.

After Gmail confirms a send, the local audit trail records the email context, invoice number, sender, recipient, Gmail message identifier, and time. It does not store the Google access token or a copy of the message body in that audit event. Sent messages remain subject to the connected Google account's retention and deletion controls.

Tokens and sharing

Google access tokens are short-lived and kept only in page memory. They are not written to local storage, Drive state, backups, or the repository. Data is shared with Google only when the operator connects a Google feature or sends, uploads, or downloads through it.

Rent Ledger's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your controls

  • Delete or correct tenants, invoices, and payments in Rent Ledger.
  • Export or import a complete JSON backup from Settings.
  • Delete Rent Ledger files from Google Drive and sent messages from Gmail.
  • Revoke Rent Ledger access from the connected Google Account's security settings.
  • Clear this origin's browser site data to remove its local records, backups, settings, and audit trail.

Terms

Use of this deployment is also subject to the Rent Ledger Terms of Use.

Questions

For privacy questions about a particular deployment, contact the Rent Ledger owner who operates that deployment or use the contact shown on its Google OAuth consent screen.
